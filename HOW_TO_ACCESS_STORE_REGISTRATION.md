# How to Access Store Registration

## 🏪 Multiple Ways to Register Your Store

We've added multiple entry points throughout the application so store owners can easily find and access the registration page.

---

## 📍 Access Points

### 1. **Header Navigation (Desktop)**
- **Location**: Top navigation bar, right side
- **Appearance**: Purple gradient button with "🏪 Sell on PriceWise" text
- **Visibility**: Always visible to all users (logged in or not)
- **Click**: Direct link to `/store/register`

**Screenshot Location**:
```
[Logo] [Search Bar...] [🏪 Sell on PriceWise] [Watchlist] [User Menu]
```

---

### 2. **Mobile Menu**
- **Location**: Hamburger menu (☰) on mobile devices
- **Appearance**: Menu item with "🏪 Sell on PriceWise" text and indigo background
- **Visibility**: Visible when mobile menu is opened
- **Click**: Direct link to `/store/register`

**How to Access**:
1. Tap the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-right corner
2. Look for "🏪 Sell on PriceWise" in the menu
3. Tap to go to registration page

---

### 3. **Footer - Quick Links**
- **Location**: Footer section, "Quick Links" column
- **Appearance**: Text link with "🏪 Sell on PriceWise" (bold)
- **Visibility**: Visible on all pages at the bottom
- **Click**: Direct link to `/store/register`

**Footer Structure**:
```
Quick Links:
- Home
- Categories
- Hot Deals
- All Products
- My Dashboard
- 🏪 Sell on PriceWise ← NEW!
```

---

### 4. **Homepage - Merchant CTA Section**
- **Location**: Homepage, before the final "Never Overpay Again" section
- **Appearance**: Large purple gradient card with detailed information
- **Visibility**: Visible on homepage only
- **Content**:
  - "Grow Your Business with PriceWise" headline
  - Feature list (verified badge, sponsored listings, analytics, etc.)
  - Sample metrics (₦2.5M+ revenue, 15,000+ customers, 12.5% conversion)
  - "Register Your Store" button

**How to Access**:
1. Go to homepage (`/`)
2. Scroll down past "Popular Products" section
3. Look for the large purple gradient card
4. Click "Register Your Store" button

## 📋 Registration Process

Once you access the registration page, here's what you'll do:

### Step 1: Fill Business Information
- Business Name (required)
- Logo URL (optional)
- Description
- Address
- City
- State
- Delivery Areas (comma-separated)

### Step 2: Fill Contact Information
- Contact Email (required)
- Contact Phone
- Website

### Step 3: Submit Registration
- Click "Register Store" button
- You'll be redirected to your Store Dashboard

### Step 4: Complete Your Profile (Optional)
- Go to Settings (`/store/settings`)
- Add payout details (bank information)
- Update any missing information

### Step 5: Upgrade to Premium (Optional)
- Go to Payment page (`/store/payment`)
- Choose Premium (₦25,000/month) or Enterprise (₦100,000/month)
- Get verified badge, analytics, and free ad credits

### Step 6: Create Your First Campaign
- Go to Campaign Manager (`/store/campaigns`)
- Click "New Campaign"
- Set budget, CPC, and placement
- Start promoting your products!

## 💡 Tips for Store Owners

### Before Registering
1. **Prepare your business information**:
   - Business name
   - Logo (hosted online with URL)
   - Contact email
   - Delivery areas you serve

2. **Decide on your membership level**:
   - **Free**: Basic features, no ads
   - **Premium** (₦25,000/month): Verified badge, analytics, 500 free credits
   - **Enterprise** (₦100,000/month): All premium + priority support, API access, 2000 credits

3. **Plan your first campaign**:
   - Which products to promote
   - Budget (minimum ₦100)
   - Cost per click (minimum ₦10)
   - Placement location (search top, comparison top, or homepage)

### After Registering
1. **Complete your profile** in Settings
2. **Add payout details** for receiving earnings
3. **Buy ad credits** if you're on Free plan
4. **Create your first campaign** to start getting visibility
5. **Monitor analytics** to optimize performance
